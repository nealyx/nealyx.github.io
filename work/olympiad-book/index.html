A way into olympiad mathematics

An introduction that builds from early competition mathematics toward proof-based olympiad thinking.

Expository mathematics · Book in progressIn progress

I’m writing an introduction to the USA Mathematical Olympiad, designed to take students from AMC 8 and early competition mathematics toward USAMO-level thinking.

The planned book will be available for free. The goal is to make the path into difficult mathematics easier to find.

The manuscript is in progress; there is no public download yet.
