Finding structure in disorder

Ramsey-theoretic mathematics research with Prof. William Gasarch at the University of Maryland.

William Gasarch · University of MarylandResearch

My mathematics research includes Ramsey-theoretic work and collaboration with Prof. William Gasarch at the University of Maryland.

Ramsey theory asks when a sufficiently large system must contain an ordered substructure, however its parts are arranged. That question is behind the little two-color graph on this site.

A project-specific abstract and public paper are not available here yet.

A small example

Choose any vertex of a complete graph on six vertices. At least three of its five edges share a color. If any edge between those three neighbors has that color, it completes a triangle with the chosen vertex. Otherwise, the three neighbors form a triangle in the other color.

Five vertices can avoid such a triangle: color a five-cycle blue and the remaining edges rust. Six cannot.
