06 / About

Neal, beyond
the shorthand.

HOWARD COUNTY, MARYLAND

I like problems where computation, mathematics, and physical intuition have something to say to each other.

My interests run from combinatorics and theoretical computer science to physics and language models. Sometimes that means a proof; sometimes a program; sometimes starting again with a better question.

Research, olympiads, competitive programming, and teaching are different ways of spending time with those questions. I care about making difficult ideas more accessible—and sharing the resources that help.
