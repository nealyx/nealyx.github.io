01

How much structure is inevitable?

Combinatorics+

Ramsey theory starts with a disarmingly simple idea: enough disorder forces a pattern. Connect six points and color every edge one of two colors. A triangle with three matching edges always appears.

That small certainty leads to much bigger questions about how structure emerges.
